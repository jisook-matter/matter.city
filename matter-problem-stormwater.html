Why it keeps happening

You can’t inspect your way out of a live problem.

Blockages build up between inspections and storms arrive on their own schedule. Without continuous level data, even a well-run inspection program is always one storm behind.

THE OLD WAY

Periodic pit inspections, then hope. When a storm hits a blocked pit, the road floods, calls flood in, and crews respond in the worst conditions.

WITH MATTER

SensAI watches the level in the pit continuously. Rising trends trigger alerts before overflow — crews clear the blockage in the dry, on their schedule.
